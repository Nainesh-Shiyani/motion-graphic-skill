The Pune Roots

A quiet sanctuary for mindful coffee

Brew Haven began in 2019 in the leafy lanes of Koregaon Park, created by two Pune locals tired of commercial, burnt roasts and cookie-cutter cafes.

We envisioned a warm space where the unhurried spirit of old Poona meets cutting-edge third-wave coffee science. We partner directly with small regenerative estates across Chikmagalur and Coorg, ensuring farmers earn well above fair-trade prices. Each batch of green beans is gently roasted inside our Koregaon Park micro-roastery to highlight intrinsic notes of citrus, dark cocoa, toasted hazelnut, and stone fruits.
